## Why native is better

The ring is generated by `add_pdn_ring` on `stdcell_grid` (Metal4 vertical / Metal5 horizontal, 2.0 µm, 1.7 µm spacing, 0.6 µm core offset) **before** signal routing. pdngen stitches the ring to the internal grid across all legal overlaps (98+ VDD Via4 arrays, 95+ VSS Via4 arrays, minimum 3 cuts per generated via). ACH Metal2 ports are tied to that ring with the same generated multi-cut via masters (`via2_3_3200_1200_1_3`, `via3_4_3200_2000_2_3`, `via4_5_3200_3200_3_3`).

The ECO fallback placed a Metal5 ring in the unused ACH envelope after routing and could not get distributed grid attachments.
